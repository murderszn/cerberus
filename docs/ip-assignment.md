# Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement

*This Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of August 2, 2026, by and between **Cerberus Labs Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and the undersigned founder (the "Founder").*

---

## 1. Transfer of Prior Inventions
The Founder hereby assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Company all rights, titles, and interests in any and all intellectual property, software, designs, algorithms, code, database schemas, and documentation created or developed by the Founder prior to the date hereof that relate to the business, research, or development of the Company (collectively, the "Prior Inventions"). This transfer includes, without limitation, the initial code repositories, the 151-point security examination checklist, and the agent swarm orchestration workflows.

---

## 2. Disclosure of Inventions
During their employment or service relationship with the Company, the Founder shall promptly disclose to the Company in writing all inventions, designs, software code, improvements, processes, formulas, algorithms, data, and know-how created, conceived, or first reduced to practice by the Founder, either alone or jointly with others (collectively, the "Inventions").

---

## 3. Assignment of Inventions
* **Ownership**: The Founder agrees that all Inventions shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company.
* **Assignment**: The Founder hereby assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Company, without further consideration, all right, title, and interest in and to all Inventions, including all copyrights, patents, trade secrets, and other intellectual property rights therein.
* **Work for Hire**: To the extent permitted by law, any copyrightable works created by the Founder in the course of their service to the Company shall be considered "works made for hire" for the Company.

---

## 4. Cooperation and Execution
The Founder agrees to assist the Company in securing, registering, patenting, and enforcing the Company's rights in the Inventions in any country. The Founder shall execute all documents, applications, and transfers necessary to register and protect the Company's ownership of the Inventions, even after termination of service.

---

## 5. Proprietary Information (Confidentiality)
The Founder shall hold in trust and keep strictly confidential all secret, proprietary, or confidential information belonging to the Company or its clients, partners, or vendors (the "Proprietary Information"). The Founder shall not disclose or use any Proprietary Information for their own benefit or for the benefit of any third party without the prior written consent of the Company.

---

## 6. Return of Company Property
Upon termination of service with the Company, or at any time upon request, the Founder shall immediately return to the Company all Company property, including computers, devices, records, files, source code copies, keys, and documents containing Proprietary Information or relating to the Inventions.

---

## 7. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of law principles.
