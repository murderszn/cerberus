# Ceberus — Brand and Messaging

## One-line idea
Personal AI helpers for regular people.

## Who it is for
- People who do not call themselves tech people
- People who already have too many apps and do not want another complicated tool
- People who want help, not hype

## What it does
- Automates one real task without setup speeches
- Keeps their information private and safe
- Works without constant babysitting

## What it is not
- Not a platform for developers
- Not a chatbot demo
- Not a science project

## Voice rules
- Simple sentences
- No fake tech words
- Respect their time and trust
- Explain like telling a friend
