# Tax Strategy & Registration Guide: Cerberus Labs Inc.

This document details the tax registration tasks, filing requirements, and strategy for **Cerberus Labs Inc.** as a Delaware C-Corporation operating in the US.

---

## 1. Federal Tax Requirements (IRS)
* **Employer Identification Number (EIN)**: The Corporation must obtain an EIN from the IRS by filing Form SS-4 online. This is required before opening bank accounts or payroll setup.
* **Corporate Income Tax (Form 1120)**: As a C-Corp, the Corporation must file an annual federal income tax return (Form 1120) by the 15th day of the fourth month following the close of the tax year (April 15th for calendar year corporations).
* **Estimated Taxes**: If the Corporation expects to owe tax of $500 or more, it must make quarterly estimated tax payments using Form 1120-W.

---

## 2. Delaware State Taxes
* **Delaware Franchise Tax**:
  - **Filing Deadline**: Annual report and franchise tax payment are due on or before **March 1** of each year.
  - **Calculation Method**: We use the **Assumed Par Value Capital Method** to calculate the tax, as the Authorized Shares method leads to excessively high tax rates for startups.
  - **Calculation Formula**:
    1. Assumed Par Value: Total Gross Assets divided by Total Issued Shares.
    2. Multiply the Assumed Par Value by Authorized Shares to calculate Assumed Capital.
    3. The tax rate is $400 for every $1,000,000 of Assumed Capital (minimum fee is $400, including a $50 filing fee).

---

## 3. Operational State Taxes (California / Regional)
If the Corporation establishes operations or hires remote employees in other states, it must register as a "Foreign Corporation" in those states.
* **California Franchise Tax (FTB)**:
  - **Registration**: File a Statement and Designation by Foreign Corporation (Form S&DC-S/A) with the California Secretary of State if active in the state.
  - **Annual Tax**: California imposes a minimum franchise tax of **$800** per year.
  - **Filing Deadline**: Tax payment is due by the 15th day of the 4th month of the corporate tax year.

---

## 4. Founder Tax Obligations: Section 83(b) Filing
* **Filing Requirement**: Founders purchasing restricted stock must file a Section 83(b) election with the IRS within **30 days** of stock purchase.
* **Tax Effect**: Prevents paying ordinary income taxes on the difference between the purchase price and fair market value as shares vest, substituting it for capital gains tax upon stock sale.
* **Instruction**:
  1. Mail signed election letter to the IRS service center where the founder files their personal return.
  2. Send via Certified Mail with Return Receipt Requested.
  3. Attach a copy of the election to the founder's annual personal tax return (Form 1040).
