## 2. Vesting Parameters
To align incentives and protect corporate equity, all founder stock grants are subject to a standard 4-year vesting schedule under a restricted stock purchase agreement (RSPA):
* **Start Date**: August 2, 2026.
* **Vesting Schedule**:
  - **1-Year Cliff (25%)**: 25% of the shares (750,000 shares per founder) shall vest on the first anniversary of the Start Date (August 2, 2027).
  - **Monthly Vesting (75%)**: The remaining 75% of the shares (2,250,000 shares per founder) shall vest in equal monthly installments (62,500 shares per month per founder) over the subsequent 36 months of service.
* **Unvested Shares**: If a founder terminates their relationship with the Corporation, all unvested shares are subject to repurchase by the Corporation at the original purchase price of $0.0001 per share.

---

## 3. Acceleration Provisions
To protect the founders during corporate transactions, the restricted stock purchase agreements include a **Double-Trigger Acceleration** clause:
* **Trigger 1**: A Change of Control event (acquisition, merger, or sale of substantially all assets of the Corporation).
* **Trigger 2**: Involuntary termination of the founder's service (termination without cause or resignation for good reason) within 12 months following the Change of Control.
* **Result**: Upon both triggers occurring, 100% of the founder's remaining unvested shares will accelerate and immediately vest.

## 4. IRS Section 83(b) Election Instructions
Each founder must file an IRS Section 83(b) election with the Internal Revenue Service within **30 days** of their stock purchase:
1. **Purpose**: Elects to pay income taxes on the fair market value of the stock at the time of purchase, rather than paying taxes as the stock vests over time. This prevents substantial tax liabilities as the company's valuation grows.
2. **Filing Deadline**: Must be postmarked within 30 days of the stock purchase date (e.g., if stock is purchased on August 2, 2026, the election must be postmarked by September 1, 2026).
3. **Mailing Address**: Send via Certified Mail with Return Receipt Requested to the IRS Center where the founder files their personal tax return.
4. **Corporate Records**: Copy of the filed 83(b) election and certified receipt must be delivered to the Corporate Secretary for inclusion in the corporate records.
