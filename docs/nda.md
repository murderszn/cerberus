# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of August 2, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between **Cerberus Labs Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at the address of record, and the counterparty signing below (each a "Party", and collectively the "Parties").

---

## 1. Purpose
The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship or transaction (the "Purpose"). In connection with the Purpose, either Party may disclose to the other Party certain proprietary, non-public, or confidential information.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any information, technical data, or know-how disclosed by the disclosing Party ("Discloser") to the receiving Party ("Recipient") that is designated as confidential or proprietary, or that under the circumstances should reasonably be understood to be confidential. Confidential Information includes, without limitation:
* Source code, database structures, security agent rules, 151-point checklists, scoring algorithms, and product roadmaps.
* Sales data, financial projections, business plans, cap tables, and founder operating structures.
* Customer lists, pricing strategies, and marketing materials.

## 3. Exclusions from Confidentiality Obligations
Confidential Information does not include information that Recipient can prove:
* Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by Recipient.
* Was already in the lawful possession of Recipient prior to disclosure by Discloser.
* Is rightfully received by Recipient from a third party without restriction on disclosure.
* Is independently developed by Recipient without reference to Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 4. Obligations of Recipient
Recipient agrees:
* To hold Discloser's Confidential Information in strict confidence and take reasonable precautions to protect it (at least as protective as those taken for its own confidential information).
* Not to use Discloser's Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Purpose defined herein.
* Not to disclose Discloser's Confidential Information to any third party without Discloser's prior written consent, except to employees, consultants, or advisors who need to know and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as strict as this Agreement.

## 5. Permitted Disclosures (Compelled Disclosure)
If Recipient is required by law, regulation, or court order to disclose any of Discloser's Confidential Information, Recipient shall, to the extent permitted by law, provide Discloser with prompt written notice of such requirement so that Discloser may seek a protective order or other remedy.

## 6. Term and Termination
This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of one (1) year from the Effective Date, or until terminated by either Party upon thirty (30) days' written notice. Recipient's obligations under this Agreement with respect to Confidential Information disclosed during the term shall survive for a period of three (3) years from the date of disclosure.

## 7. Return or Destruction of Materials
Upon Discloser's written request, Recipient shall immediately return or destroy all copies of Discloser's Confidential Information and certify such destruction in writing to Discloser.

## 8. No License or Warranty
All Confidential Information remains the sole property of Discloser. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting Recipient any license, patent, copyright, or intellectual property rights. All Confidential Information is provided "AS IS" without warranty of any kind.

## 9. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflicts of law principles. Any legal action arising under this Agreement shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.
