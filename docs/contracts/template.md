# Master Services Agreement (MSA) Template

*This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of the date of execution (the "Effective Date") by and between **Cerberus Labs Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("Provider"), and the client executing a Statement of Work ("Client").*

---

## 1. Scope of Services
Provider shall perform security examination and monitoring services as described in one or more Statements of Work (each an "SOW") executed by the Parties. Each SOW shall reference this Agreement and incorporate its terms.

## 2. Client Responsibilities & Authorization
* **Access Permissions**: Client shall provide Provider with secure read-only access to source code repositories, staging servers, host URLs, and API endpoints necessary to perform the examinations.
* **Authorization**: Client represents and warrants that it owns or possesses all necessary permissions to authorize Provider to scan, test, and perform vulnerability analysis on all target assets.

## 3. Fees & Payment Terms
* **Fees**: Client agrees to pay all fees specified in the SOW.
* **Billing**: One-time audit fees are invoiced upon execution of the SOW and must be paid before work commences. Continuous monitoring subscriptions are invoiced monthly in advance.
* **Late Payments**: Unpaid invoices older than thirty (30) days will accumulate interest at a rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law.

## 4. Confidentiality & Intellectual Property
* **Mutual Confidentiality**: Each Party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other Party, including source code, credentials, and vulnerability reports.
* **Client IP**: Client retains all right, title, and interest in its source code, repositories, and applications.
* **Provider IP**: Provider retains all right, title, and interest in its security agents, 151-point specification database, scoring algorithms, and report templates.

## 5. Term & Termination
* **Term**: This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and remain in effect until terminated by either Party.
* **Termination for Convenience**: Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' written notice, provided that all active SOWs have been completed or cancelled.
* **Termination for Cause**: Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other Party commits a material breach and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days of receiving written notice.

## 6. Liability Disclaimers & Limits
* **Vulnerability Assessment Limitation**: Client acknowledges that automated security examinations are diagnostic tools and do not guarantee the identification of all security threats. Provider does not warrant that Client's systems will be entirely secure or invulnerable to malicious attacks.
* **Limitation of Liability**: PROVIDER'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT, OR OTHERWISE, SHALL BE LIMITED TO THE FEES PAID BY CLIENT TO PROVIDER UNDER THE SPECIFIC SOW GIVING RISE TO THE LIABILITY IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

## 7. Miscellaneous
* **Governing Law**: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
* **Arbitration**: Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved via binding arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware.
