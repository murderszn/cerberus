# Operating Agreement: Cerberus Labs LLC

*This Operating Agreement is entered into by and among the founding members of Cerberus Labs LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, effective as of August 2, 2026.*

---

## 1. Formation & Purpose
* **Entity**: The Members have formed Cerberus Labs LLC by filing the Certificate of Formation with the Delaware Secretary of State.
* **Purpose**: The company's primary purpose is to research, build, and distribute autonomous AI agent security auditing software, including point-in-time security audits and continuous code telemetry monitoring tools.

---

## 2. Membership & Management Structure
* **Management**: The company is manager-managed by its founders. The initial Managers are:
  - **Joshua Johnson**: Chief Executive Officer & Chief Financial Officer (Corporate strategy, capital allocation, banking, sales ops)
  - **Caleb Johnson**: Chief Operating Officer (Swarm engineering, orchestrator execution, product delivery)
  - **Elijah Johnson**: Chief Technology Officer (Zero-trust runtimes, WASM sandboxing, protocol specifications)
* **Voting Rights**: Major corporate decisions (including equity dilution, debt issuance, IP transfers, or company dissolution) require a majority vote of the managers. Day-to-day operations are handled within respective officer scopes.

---

## 3. Capital Contributions & Equity Vesting
* **Capital Contributions**: The initial capital contributions and equity ownership stakes are established as follows:
  - Joshua Johnson: 33.3% Membership Interest
  - Caleb Johnson: 33.3% Membership Interest
  - Elijah Johnson: 33.3% Membership Interest
* **Vesting Schedule**: To ensure long-term alignment, founder equity is subject to a standard 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff:
  - 25% of each founder's equity vests on the one-year anniversary of the start date.
  - The remaining 75% vests in equal monthly installments over the following 36 months.
  - Unvested equity is subject to repurchase by the company upon founder departure.

---

## 4. Intellectual Property Assignment
* **IP Transfer**: As a condition of membership, each Member assigns and transfers all rights, titles, and interests in any software code, designs, algorithms, architectures, brand names, or technical specifications developed for Cerberus Labs (including the 151-point inspection matrix and agent configurations) to Cerberus Labs LLC.
* **Work for Hire**: All future technical or business intellectual property created during membership is designated as "work for hire" owned solely by the company.

---

## 5. Buy-Sell Provisions & Dissolution
* **Transfer Restriction**: No Member may sell, assign, or transfer their Membership Interest to an outside party without the unanimous written consent of the remaining Members.
* **Right of First Refusal**: If a Member wishes to sell their vested interest, the company and remaining Members hold the first right to purchase those shares at fair market value.
* **Dissolution**: The company may be dissolved only by unanimous agreement of the Members. Upon dissolution, all company assets and IP will be liquidated to settle outstanding liabilities, with the remainder distributed according to final capital account balances.
