This document outlines the corporate formation checklist for establishing **Cerberus Labs Inc.** as a Delaware C-Corporation.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Formation Checklist

### Phase 1: Initial Filing (Delaware State)
- [ ] **Name Reservation**: Confirm availability of "Cerberus Labs Inc." with the Delaware Division of Corporations.
- [ ] **Filing of Certificate of Incorporation**: File the Certificate of Incorporation with the Delaware Division of Corporations.
- [ ] **Registered Agent Setup**: Finalize agreement with Delaware Registered Agent.
- [ ] **Filing Fee Payment**: Settle Delaware state filing fees (standard $89 filing fee + optional expedited processing).

### Phase 2: Post-Incorporation Setup (Federal & Corporate)
- [ ] **Obtain Employer Identification Number (EIN)**: File Form SS-4 with the IRS to obtain a federal tax identification number.
- [ ] **Adopt Corporate Bylaws**: Adopt the initial bylaws of the corporation to establish governance guidelines.
- [ ] **Sole Incorporator Actions**: Sign the Action of Sole Incorporator, appointing the initial Board of Directors and resigning as Incorporator.
- [ ] **Board of Directors Meeting / Written Consent**:
  - [ ] Appoint initial officers (Joshua Johnson as CEO/CFO, Caleb Johnson as COO, Elijah Johnson as CTO).
  - [ ] Approve founder stock purchase agreements (restricted stock grants).
  - [ ] Adopt corporate seal and stock certificate templates.
  - [ ] Select fiscal year end (December 31).
  - [ ] Authorize the opening of corporate bank accounts.

### Phase 3: Equity Issuance & Founder Compliance
- [ ] **Founder Stock Purchase Agreements**: Execute formal restricted stock purchase agreements for all founders.
- [ ] **File 83(b) Elections**: File Form 83(b) with the IRS within 30 days of stock issuance. Maintain certified mail receipts.
- [ ] **State Securities Filings (Blue Sky Laws)**: File necessary state securities notices (e.g., California Section 25102(f) notice) for initial stock issuances.
- [ ] **Intellectual Property Assignment**: Execute Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreements (PIIA) transferring existing Cerberus codebase and assets to the corporation.
